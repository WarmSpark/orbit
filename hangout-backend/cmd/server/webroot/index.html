Your privacy isn't the product.

Orbit is designed so you can meet people without giving up your data. We collect the minimum, blur the rest, and let plans expire on their own.

  • ✓Fuzzed location. Pins are offset by ~110m so no one can find your doorstep.
  • ✓Hidden identity. Your email is never shown to other users — only the host sees who joined.
  • ✓Auto-expiry. Every hangout and its chat disappears 24 hours after it's created.
  • ✓No tracking feed. No ads, no follower graph, no behavioural profiling.
